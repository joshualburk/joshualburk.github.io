Welcome to Essetara

Essetara is a dark fantasy world where power flows through Essence, the hidden energy of all living things. Those who learn to harness it can Manifest supernatural abilities, and rival factions now clash to control it.

The Lost Hour

It began with a brilliant, blinding flash of light, followed by absolute darkness. Those who experienced it recall being unable to move, as though frozen in time. Then, as suddenly as it began, another brilliant flash of light restored their vision.

But how much time had passed? A minute? An hour? A day?

Some reported finding fresh fruit rotten and recently cut flowers wilted and nearly dry. Others found their drinks still filled with ice and their soup as hot as when it had been served. Even clocks offered no answers. Some had advanced by mere seconds, while others had advanced by hours or even days. Clocks within the same room displayed vastly different times. No one could explain the inconsistencies, and no one could definitively establish how long the darkness had lasted.

It would come to be known as the Lost Hour.

Despite the countless conflicting accounts of what happened, there is one thing upon which everyone agrees.

Essetara changed that day.
