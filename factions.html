Order of Absolution

Destroy the Sites of Wonder

Before the Event, the Order of Absolution was little more than a fringe group of protesters who warned against visiting the Sites of Wonder. When the Event occurred, they saw it as confirmation of their warnings and humanity's subsequent embrace of Essence as a sin. Now a powerful religious order, they seek to destroy every Site of Wonder, severing the flow of Essence and restoring Essetara to its rightful state. Their Manifesters, known as the Bearers, are worshipped as living saints for willingly sacrificing their own purity so that humanity may achieve absolution. Unknown to most members, the Order's highest leaders believe true absolution can only be achieved through the eradication of ALL those tainted by Essence...

Esse's Chosen

Protect the Sites of Wonder

Prior to the Event, Aldren Voss was a well-known figure in the public eye. Following the Event, he claimed to have received a vision of a mysterious being, whom he named Esse, that charged him with protecting the Sites of Wonder and preserving the natural flow of Essence. Drawing upon his public recognition and influence, he quickly amassed a following and established an organization known as Esse's Chosen. While most members now regard Aldren as somewhat eccentric, few question the importance of his cause. Whether guided by a higher being or the delusions of a once-respected man, Esse's Chosen remain committed to protecting the Sites from those who seek to exploit, control, or destroy them.

New Horizon Industries

Exploit the Potential of Essence

Prior to the Event, Horizon Industries was an established company specializing in resource extraction and technological development. Recognizing the commercial potential of Essence, the company quickly redirected its resources toward developing a device capable of extracting, storing, and transporting it. This technological breakthrough transformed the company into one of the most economically influential organizations in Essetara, prompting its rebranding as New Horizon Industries. While the company promotes the benefits Essence could bring to society, its growing demand for extraction sites has placed it at odds with those seeking to protect, control, or destroy them. To New Horizon Industries, Essence is neither a blessing nor a curse; it is an opportunity.
