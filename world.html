World of Essetara

Long before Essence transformed civilization, Essetara was a world of familiar landscapes and extraordinary mysteries. Everything changed during an event that would become known as the Lost Hour.

Before the Lost Hour

The Sites of Wonder

Long before the Lost Hour, scattered throughout Essetara were mysterious locations known as the Sites of Wonder.

These locations were home to strange but seemingly harmless phenomena that defied conventional explanation. Shadows pointed toward the sun, objects returned to an upright position when knocked over, and flames burned without producing heat.

For generations, the Sites attracted curious travelers, researchers, and those who simply wished to witness the extraordinary.

The Lost Hour

It began with a brilliant, blinding flash of light, followed by absolute darkness. Those who experienced it recall being unable to move, as though frozen in time. Then, as suddenly as it began, another brilliant flash of light restored their vision.

But how much time had passed? A minute? An hour? A day?

Some reported finding fresh fruit rotten and recently cut flowers wilted and nearly dry. Others found their drinks still filled with ice and their soup as hot as when it had been served.

Even clocks offered no answers. Some had advanced by mere seconds, while others had advanced by hours or even days. Clocks within the same room displayed vastly different times.

No one could explain the inconsistencies, and no one could definitively establish how long the darkness had lasted.

It would come to be known as the Lost Hour.

Despite the countless conflicting accounts of what happened, there is one thing upon which everyone agrees.

Essetara changed that day.

After the Lost Hour

The Sites Transformed

Following the Lost Hour, these once harmless peculiarities became far more pronounced. Stones floated above the ground, vegetation grew at astonishing rates, and bodies of water exhibited properties that defied the known laws of nature.

What had once been little more than curiosities could no longer be dismissed as such.

The Discovery of Essence

As researchers began investigating these changes, they discovered a mysterious force that would come to be known as Essence.

Further investigation revealed that the Sites were not isolated phenomena but were interconnected through a vast network of Essence flowing through the air, waterways, and beneath the surface of Essetara.

Smaller manifestations began appearing throughout the world where these flows surfaced or converged.

The Manifesters

Perhaps the most remarkable discovery, however, was that certain individuals had developed the ability to sense and manipulate Essence.

These individuals would become known as Manifesters.

Whether Essence had always existed or was brought into existence by the Lost Hour remains a subject of debate. What is certain is that its discovery would forever change the balance of power in Essetara.
